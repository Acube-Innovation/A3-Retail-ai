# Catalogue artwork credits

Two kinds of picture live here.

`*.svg` — flat drawings made for this app. They cover the things a photograph
cannot show usefully at thumbnail size (a protection plan, an hour of labour)
and stand in wherever no photograph is mapped. Same licence as the app.

`photos/*.jpg` — real photographs, cropped and resized for the counter grid.
Every one is under a licence that permits commercial use and modification, and
each is credited below. Nothing under CC BY-ND is used here, because cropping
a picture to a thumbnail is a derivative work.

These are demo assets. A shop that uploads its own product photography
overwrites them — `demo.images` never touches an item that already has one.
